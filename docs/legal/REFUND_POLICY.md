## 1. 7-Day Free Trial Policy

1. **Zero-Risk Evaluation:** Eligible new subscribers may receive a 7-day introductory free trial to evaluate premium Deutsch Pfad features (Pro or Pro Plus).
2. **Cancellation Prior to Trial Expiry:** You may cancel your trial at any moment during the 7-day period directly through the Billing Portal in your Account Settings. If cancelled before the trial ends, **you will not be charged**, and your access will revert to the complimentary Free Tier at the end of the trial period.
3. **Transition to Paid Subscription:** If you do not cancel prior to the exact date and time of trial conclusion, your trial will automatically convert to the recurring billing cycle selected during signup (Monthly or Annual), and your payment method will be charged accordingly.

---

## 2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee & EU Right of Withdrawal

We want you to be completely satisfied with your German learning journey.

1. **Initial Paid Subscriptions:** If you subscribe to a paid monthly or annual subscription and decide that Deutsch Pfad does not suit your study needs, you may request a **100% full refund within fourteen (14) calendar days** of the initial transaction.
2. **EU / EEA Consumer Statutory Right of Withdrawal:** Pursuant to EU Directive 2011/83/EU, consumers resident in the European Union and European Economic Area have the legal right to withdraw from distance contracts within 14 days without providing grounds. If you have commenced using digital features during this period, you remain entitled to a prompt refund upon notice.
3. **Annual Plan Consideration:** For annual subscriptions, refund requests submitted after the initial 14-day window may be eligible for a pro-rated credit or discretionary exception in cases of technical non-availability, assessed on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 3. Recurring Renewals & Grace Period

1. **Cancellation Anytime:** You may cancel recurring auto-renewal at any time. When cancelled, your account retains premium access through the remainder of your paid billing term.
2. **Accidental Renewal Grace Period:** If your subscription renewed automatically and you did not intend to continue, you may contact billing@deutschpfad.com within **forty-eight (48) hours** of the renewal charge. Provided you have not engaged in heavy usage (e.g., taking multiple mock exams or extensive AI evaluations) following renewal, we will gladly issue a full refund and cancel the term.

## 4. Refund Eligibility Exclusions

Refunds will not be issued under the following circumstances:
- Requests submitted beyond the 14-day initial purchase guarantee window (except as mandated by applicable statutory law).
- Account suspensions or terminations resulting from intentional violations of our Acceptable Use Policy (e.g., prompt injection attacks, scraping, or credential sharing).
- Purchases completed through third-party app stores (Apple App Store or Google Play Store), where billing and refunds are governed strictly by the respective store's refund procedures.

---

## 5. How to Submit a Refund Request

To initiate a refund, please provide your account details:

1. **Email:** Send a request to **billing@deutschpfad.com** with the subject line `Refund Request - [Your Registered Email]`.
2. **Details Required:**
   - Your account email address
   - Transaction reference ID (from your Stripe, Razorpay, or Paddle receipt)
   - Reason for refund (optional, but greatly appreciated to help us improve the platform)
3. **Self-Service Billing Portal:** You can also trigger cancellation and invoice review directly via [Settings > Billing Portal](https://deutschpfad.com/settings/billing).

---

## 6. Processing Times & Disbursement

Approved refunds are processed immediately by our billing department and transmitted to our payment gateway. Funds typically reflect in your bank account or on your credit card statement within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on your financial institution. All refunds are disbursed to the original payment method used during checkout.
